## Ujjwal Choraria

CS undergrad at **Shiv Nadar University, Chennai**, working at the intersection of machine

---

### What I'm building

**[capitalsense-engine](https://github.com/Ujjwal-6/capitalsense-engine)** — A deterministic
cash-flow decision engine for small businesses. Four engines take a cash position and
produce nine ranked payment strategies with plain-language reasoning: health scoring →
three-scenario risk projection → payment optimisation → explainability. 73 tests, zero ML,
fully auditable. *This is the piece of work I'd most want someone to read.*

**[agritech-platform](https://github.com/Ujjwal-6/agritech-platform)** — Crop intelligence
that tracks where a crop actually is, not where a calendar says it should be. Pulls NASA
POWER satellite weather, accumulates Growing Degree Days, resolves phenological stage, and
gates advisories on it — so it won't tell a farmer to spray during flowering. Seven rule
engines plus XGBoost ranking.

**[converge-collaborate](https://github.com/Ujjwal-6/converge-collaborate)** — Resume-to-project
matching using Gaussian Mixture soft clustering. Every match returns its full score
breakdown, because a ranking you can't interrogate isn't one anyone should trust.
